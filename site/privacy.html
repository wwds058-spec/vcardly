VCardly Privacy Policy

Effective date: [DATE YOU PUBLISH] · App: VCardly (Android, package com.yasin.vcardly)

In short

  • Your contacts, card photos, notes and follow-ups are stored only on your phone. VCardly has no account and no server, and never uploads them.
  • Card text is read on your phone. The card image and the text are never sent anywhere.
  • If a version of VCardly shows ads (free plan, one banner from Google AdMob), Google may use your device's advertising ID for them. VCardly Pro has no ads.
  • Payments are handled by Google Play. VCardly never sees your payment details.

Information stored on your device

Everything you add to VCardly is kept in the app's private storage on your phone, where other apps cannot read it:

  • contacts and the details you enter or scan (names, job titles, companies, phone numbers, email addresses, websites, addresses, notes);
  • photos of visiting cards;
  • categories, tags, follow-ups and reminders;
  • your own digital card ("My card") and your settings.

We do not collect, receive or have access to any of this information. Android's automatic cloud backup is switched off for VCardly, so the system does not upload it either.

Reading visiting cards

When you scan a card, the text is recognised on your phone by Google's ML Kit library. The card image and the recognised text stay on the phone and are never stored or logged beyond what you choose to save. Like other Google libraries, ML Kit may send Google anonymous diagnostic and performance information (for example device model, Android version and how long recognition took). It never includes the image or the text. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure for details.

Ads (free plan)

The current version of VCardly shows no ads and does not use the advertising ID. If a later version's free plan shows ads, it shows one banner ad from Google AdMob on the Home screen. VCardly does not give the ad network any information about your contacts. Google may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, and ad interaction data to show, measure and limit ads, as described in Google's advertising policies. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK), you are asked for consent first and can change your choice in the app's Settings. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. VCardly Pro removes ads.

Purchases

VCardly Pro is sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment under Google's Privacy Policy. The app only receives a confirmation that the purchase is valid; it never sees your card or bank details.

Sharing, exports and backups

Nothing leaves your phone unless you choose to send it. When you share a contact as a QR code or contact file, you choose which details are included, and private notes are never offered. Exports (contact file, PDF, CSV, Excel) and backup files are saved where you choose. A backup can be protected with a password, which encrypts it (AES-256). We cannot recover a forgotten backup password.

Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. You can delete individual contacts, erase all data from Settings, or uninstall the app, which removes its data from the phone. Backup or export files you saved elsewhere are not affected; delete them where you saved them.

Children

VCardly is a business tool intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Security

Data is protected by Android's app sandbox and device encryption. Optional App lock asks for your fingerprint, face or device PIN, and screen privacy hides the app's content from the recent-apps view and screenshots.
